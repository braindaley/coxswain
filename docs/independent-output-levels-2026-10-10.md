# Independent measured effort colors

All effort legends use Light blue, Moderate green, Vigorous gold, and Peak red. Measured heart rate retains its own frozen personal HR zones. Power and pace now have separate frozen relative-output thresholds. Stroke-rate traces use a neutral color because cadence alone cannot classify effort. Programmed interval bands retain their prescribed effort colors; they do not determine the measured line color.

Settings → Hardware → Power and pace levels suggests editable thresholds from the most recent 20 completed workouts. Suggestions exclude rest, unavailable/untimed measurements and the existing initial-sensor-anomaly window. The median positive value in each five-second bin supplies one observation, with at least two readings per bin and twelve bins per metric required. Power boundaries use the 25th, 50th and 85th percentiles; pace uses the corresponding inverse split-time ranks (75th, 50th and 15th). Flat history that cannot produce three distinct boundaries does not fabricate levels. These are explicitly relative performance categories, not physiological zones or prescribed training intensity.

Blank settings initially receive suggestions; existing saved settings and manually edited forms remain unchanged unless the user chooses Use history suggestions. Power fields increase from Moderate to Peak. Split fields decrease, accept m:ss per 500 m or integer seconds, and save in seconds. All three fields for a metric can be cleared to leave it unclassified. Save is disabled for partial, invalid or reversed limits. A disabled profile affects future recordings only.

Database version 9 adds nullable Workout.performanceZones. New recordings freeze the profile at their first measurement, including both pace and power thresholds independently. JSON backups preserve and validate both the current setting and each recording’s frozen profile before replacing history. Older workouts keep null stored thresholds. Results now use current settings for these rows when available, with an explicit current-settings note. Recorded profiles always take precedence, and stored data is unchanged.

The graph divides a line segment exactly where its own measurement crosses a boundary, in either direction. Lower split times enter higher levels. A slowdown can therefore turn pace or power blue while elevated HR stays red, making recovery differences visible. Numerical thresholds and labels accompany the colors; existing interval breaks, sensor-gap handling, clocks, averages and goals are preserved.
