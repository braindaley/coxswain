# Five heart-rate training zones and live tile

New heart-rate profiles use five zones based on maximum heart rate, with increasing lower boundaries at 60%, 70%, 80% and 90%. This follows the common five-zone maximum-HR convention described by [Polar](https://www.polar.com/us-en/guide/heart-rate-zones). Existing maximum-based or reserve settings with a known maximum automatically supply that maximum for new profiles. Resting heart rate is retained when available but no longer changes the training boundaries. A history-observed peak remains an estimate, not a tested maximum; the settings retain this explanation and allow editing or an age-based estimate.

Settings support four custom boundaries for Zones 2–5. Old custom profiles without a maximum cannot be unambiguously converted and require the additional boundary in Settings. The version-two encoding includes Zone 5 and preserves version-one decoding for saved history and backups. Legacy recorded profiles remain four-zone profiles; future sessions freeze the new five-zone definition. Data samples, totals and race scores are unchanged.

The live heart-rate tile shows actual BPM even when a heart-rate goal would otherwise supply a variance. Its label is “Zone 2 · BPM”, for example, and its background changes with the zone. The palette is slate blue, teal, dark gold, green and burnt orange, with white text meeting at least 4.5:1 contrast. The zone number is always present so color is not the only identifier. Missing/zero readings have no zone label or zone background. Below 50% of a configured maximum, the tile says “Below Zone 1” with a neutral background; history combines those readings into the lowest bucket and explains that in the info dialog. Five-zone history uses five guides, colors and time buckets. Power, pace and cadence keep their independent row-relative output bands.

Unit verification covers exact boundary membership, serialization, legacy conversion, custom thresholds, segment crossing and color contrast. UI verification covers live changes, missing HR, actual BPM rather than goal variance, five-zone settings, dark mode and legacy/current history.

Validation completed: production debug build, 185 unit tests and 12 isolated emulator screen checks passed. The two live-tile checks also assert rendered background pixels in light/dark mode, including precedence over optional goal colors; the Zone 2 tile image was visually inspected. White tile text meets at least 4.5:1 contrast in all five backgrounds. Tests use the separate review application and do not install over or modify phone data.
