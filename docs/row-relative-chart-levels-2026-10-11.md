# Row-relative output charts

Workout results now derive split, power and cadence colors from that workout’s moving trace, independent of personal output settings or its saved output profile. Heart rate continues using personal physiological zones and its existing time-in-zone summary.

The central 5th–95th percentile range of the ten-second display trace is divided into four equal-width output bands. These are Low, Medium, High and Highest, with blue, teal, gold and green. Pace reverses the boundaries because lower split is faster. Nearly flat or sparse rows do not invent four bands. The axis includes valid plotted readings and the new guides, without adding workout averages or prescribed targets to non-heart-rate scales. Colors are relative within one workout and cannot compare effort between workouts. Original recordings, totals, coaching references, saved settings and race scoring are unchanged.

Split filtering recognizes zero power even when cadence remains stale, provided the device records positive power elsewhere. Devices without power retain valid pace. During the minute after an idle or recorded pause, speed is compared with five seconds of cumulative distance movement. A speed reading below 70% of that movement is hidden until the readings agree again. The comparison uses following samples for the first resumed strokes and preceding samples thereafter; it stays within the same interval. Genuine slow rowing with consistent counters is retained. Stored measurements and statistics are unchanged.
